# Natural Writing Review

Use this shared editorial standard for every release-related prose artifact. It is an adaptation of the heuristics in [AI Writing Detection](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/blob/main/skills/seo-audit/references/ai-writing-detection.md), not a promise that any detector can classify the result.

## Scope

Apply this review to every authored or substantially rewritten release artifact, including:

- Chinese and English product documentation;
- `README.md`, `README.en.md`, guides, reference pages, and onboarding copy;
- root and bundled-server changelogs;
- release notes, highlights, and publication copy;
- user-visible notes added to release handoff material.

Do not apply it by mechanically replacing every listed word. Preserve precise product terminology, commands, paths, version numbers, API names, and risk disclosures. Revise repeated or clustered patterns while keeping the copy accurate and natural.

## Editing Checklist

- Lead with a concrete subject, an active verb, and a visible user or contributor outcome. Replace abstract claims with the specific action or result the reader will notice.
- Treat em dashes as rare punctuation. Prefer commas, colons, or parentheses, and revise any paragraph that uses more than one.
- Avoid clusters of ornate verbs such as `delve`, `leverage`, `utilise`, `facilitate`, `foster`, `bolster`, `underscore`, `unveil`, `navigate`, `streamline`, or `enhance` when plain verbs such as `use`, `help`, `show`, `improve`, or `manage` are accurate.
- Avoid stacked promotional adjectives such as `robust`, `comprehensive`, `pivotal`, `crucial`, `transformative`, `cutting-edge`, `groundbreaking`, `innovative`, `seamless`, `nuanced`, or `holistic`. Keep an adjective only when it adds verifiable information.
- Remove generic openings, transitions, and conclusions such as “In today's...”, “In an era of...”, “It's important to note...”, “That being said...”, “With that in mind...”, “In conclusion...”, and “To sum up...”. Start with the product, action, constraint, or result instead.
- Rewrite formulaic structures such as “Whether you're X, Y, or Z...”, “It's not just X, it's also Y...”, elaborate “Think of X as...” metaphors, and repeated “By + gerund” openings.
- Remove empty intensifiers and filler words such as `absolutely`, `actually`, `basically`, `clearly`, `definitely`, `essentially`, `incredibly`, `really`, `significantly`, `simply`, `truly`, and `very` unless they change the meaning.
- Prefer conversational alternatives to academic filler such as `shed light on`, `pave the way for`, `a myriad of`, `a plethora of`, `paramount`, `prior to`, `subsequent to`, `in light of`, `with respect to`, and `in terms of`.

## Final Pass

Read the prose aloud, or simulate that test carefully when the language is unfamiliar. Check for repeated sentence shapes, similar sentence lengths, clusters of listed terms, and intensifiers that contribute no meaning. Vary sentence rhythm without changing the meaning, scope, or risk disclosure between Chinese and English.
